Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

FretAttack is operated by Caleb Roach.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the FretAttack website and application.

Website: https://www.fretattackapp.com

Contact: fretattackapp@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

Users can access Free features without creating an account.

Creating an account is required for Pro access.

When you create an account, we collect or process information such as:

  • Your email address.
  • Your unique account identifier.
  • Email verification and authentication information.
  • Your Free or Pro membership status.

Account authentication and membership records are managed through Supabase.

Your password is handled by the authentication provider and is not stored by FretAttack in readable form.

Payment Information

FretAttack Pro purchases will be processed through Stripe when payment functionality is enabled.

Stripe may collect payment card details, billing information, and other information needed to complete a transaction.

FretAttack may receive transaction identifiers, payment confirmation, and information needed to provide purchased Pro access.

FretAttack does not directly store complete credit or debit card numbers.

Locally Stored Information

FretAttack uses browser storage, including localStorage and IndexedDB, for certain application features.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Instrument and tuning preferences.
  • Selected scales, keys, and modes.
  • Practice progress and preferences.
  • Songwriting drafts and saved songs.
  • Saved riffs and associated audio.
  • Display and accessibility preferences.

This information is generally stored on the device and browser where it was created.

It is not automatically synchronized between devices unless a cloud synchronization feature is specifically introduced.

3. Microphone Access

FretAttack's Hum a Riff feature may request permission to use your device's microphone.

Microphone access is used to analyze audio and identify musical pitches.

In the current implementation, microphone analysis and saved riff recordings are handled locally in your browser rather than uploaded to FretAttack's servers.

Saved recordings may remain in your browser's IndexedDB storage until deleted or cleared.

If FretAttack introduces cloud audio processing or storage in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and verify email addresses.
  • Provide password recovery.
  • Determine Free or Pro membership access.
  • Process and verify purchases.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain the functionality and security of the Service.
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

FretAttack uses browser storage and authentication mechanisms to maintain sessions, remember settings, and support application functionality.

Browser storage may contain locally saved musical projects and preferences.

Clearing browser data may remove locally saved work and require you to sign in again.

Any optional analytics, advertising tracking, or nonessential cookies must be accurately disclosed before they are enabled.

7. Information Sharing

We share information with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the Service, authenticate accounts, process payments, provide support, or meet legal obligations.

We may also disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the Service and its users.

We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Retention

Account information is generally retained while your account remains active.

After account deletion, certain records may be retained where necessary for accounting, tax compliance, fraud prevention, payment disputes, legal obligations, or purchase restoration.

Locally saved songs, riffs, recordings, and preferences remain subject to your browser's storage behavior.

Deleting an online account does not necessarily delete locally saved content from your device.

9. Account Deletion and Your Choices

You may request account deletion by contacting fretattackapp@gmail.com.

When the in-app deletion feature is implemented, you will also be able to initiate account deletion through Settings.

The planned process will include:

  1. An initial explanation and confirmation.
  2. A randomly generated four-digit code displayed in the application, which you must correctly enter before proceeding with permanent deletion.

For security, account deletion will also require appropriate identity verification and secure server-side processing.

The four-digit confirmation code is intended to prevent accidental deletion, not to replace account authentication.

You may also:

  • Use Free features without creating an account.
  • Request access to or correction of eligible personal information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Control microphone access through your browser or device.
  • Clear locally stored information through browser settings.

We may verify your identity before processing requests involving personal account information.

10. Data Security

We use established service providers and reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect account and membership information.

Authentication and membership information are managed through Supabase.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their passwords and avoid sharing their accounts.
